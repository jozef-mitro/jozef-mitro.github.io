    Weather Flower Generator

    Choose a season and forecast length. The first day starts at the hex selected by a 2d6 roll; each later day moves one hex according to the movement roll.

    Rolls of 9 or 10 repeat the current weather. If a move hits a marked line, the weather repeats. An unmarked edge wraps to the opposite end of the same row. Exclamation marks indicate severe or hindering conditions.

    Movement key: 2 or 12: northwest; 3 or 4: southwest; 5 or 6: south; 7: southeast; 8: northeast; 9 or 10: repeat; 11: north.
